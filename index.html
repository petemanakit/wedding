Attire

Black tie throughout

Vow ceremony · 4.30 PM

Black tie, the same as the evening, so that we move from the lawn to the ballroom without a change of dress. The lawn is grass, so a block heel is kinder than a stiletto.

Gentlemen

Black tie means a tuxedo: a dinner jacket with satin or grosgrain lapels, a bow tie and a white dress shirt. A dark lounge suit with a black necktie is not quite the same, and we would love to see you in the real thing.

Ladies

Elegant evening gown, floor length preferred. January evenings by the river are pleasantly cool, around 24 °C, so a wrap is welcome but not essential.

How to arrive

By car
Enter from Charoen Krung Road at Soi 36 (Trok Rong Phasi), beside the French Embassy. Valet parking will be available at the hotel entrance.
By BTS
Saphan Taksin station (Silom line), then five minutes by taxi, or a short walk along Charoen Krung Road.
From BKK
Suvarnabhumi Airport is about 45 minutes by car outside peak hours; Don Mueang about an hour.

January in Bangkok

The best month of the year: dry, with days around 31 °C and evenings near 24 °C. Sunset on the 17th is at about 6.10 PM, just as the reception begins.

Visiting from abroad

Most passports enter Thailand visa-free for tourism. Grab (ride-hailing) works throughout the city, and English is widely spoken at the hotels. If you would like help with a longer stay, from Chiang Mai to the islands, we are only too happy to suggest.

Good to know

Questions

❦
What should I wear?

Black tie throughout: a tuxedo for gentlemen and an elegant evening gown for ladies, for the rehearsal dinner, the vow ceremony and the evening alike. The Schedule page has a little more detail.

May I bring a guest?

Your invitation is addressed to the guests we are able to welcome. If a plus-one is included, the reply form will show a space for their name. Do write to us if you are unsure.

Are children welcome?

We adore your little ones, but the evening celebration is for grown-ups so that everyone may relax and stay late. Please let us know if this creates any difficulty.

Gifts

Your presence, in Bangkok and in black tie, is the gift. For those who insist, a traditional envelope may be offered at the reception; there is no registry.

Where do I park?

Valet parking will be available at The Langham entrance on Charoen Krung Soi 36. Taxis and Grab are plentiful at the end of the evening, and the hotel concierge will be glad to call one for you.

What if it rains?

January is Bangkok's driest month. Should the river surprise us, the vow ceremony will move indoors and we will let you know on arrival.

Photographs and social media

We have a wonderful photographer and ask only that phones stay away during the vows. Afterwards, please share freely; we will post our hashtag here nearer the day.

Whom do I contact?

For anything at all, write to peerakarn@ionenergy.ai. On the day itself, our planners from Wedding is "ME" will be on hand throughout.
